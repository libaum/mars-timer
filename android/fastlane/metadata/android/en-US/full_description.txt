A minimalist timer for meditation, built to disappear behind your practice. No guided voices, no gamification, no menus. Just a bell to start and a bell to end.

- Quick-select durations, editable to fit your practice, or adjust by the minute
- An optional delay before the bell, so you have time to settle in
- Keep sitting past the bell: overtime still counts toward your total
- Minutes and days meditated, no streaks. Just a quiet record, not something to keep up.
- No subscription, no account, no tracking. Nothing leaves your phone.

I meditated for years with apps that got more cluttered every update. I just wanted a timer, no streak to keep up, nothing to get lost in. Meditation happens in this sitting, not because of the last one or for the next.

Mars — Minimalist And Really Simple. A growing family of small, calm tools built around one idea: solve one problem well, and never fight for your attention. No ads, no tracking, nothing built to keep you hooked. Made by one person, out of conviction — tools that work for you, not on you.
